Welcome to the newly migrated site! Moving from heavy frameworks to a lightweight static site generator like Zola is a game-changer for speed and simplicity.

## Why We Switched

Speed, simplicity, and total control over the markup. With Zola, everything compiles into pure HTML and CSS in milliseconds.

```toml
# config.toml snippet
title = "Zola Kisse"
base_url = "[https://example.com](https://example.com)"
compile_sass = true
```
