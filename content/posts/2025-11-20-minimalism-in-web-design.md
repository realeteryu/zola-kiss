Minimalism isn't about having less; it's about making room for what actually matters. 

## The Core Principles

1. **Typography first**: Clear readable fonts like Roboto.
2. **Generous whitespace**: Let elements breathe.
3. **Purposeful interactions**: Subtle hover states, clean borders, and no unnecessary animations.

> "Perfection is achieved, not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing left to take away." — Antoine de Saint-Exupéry

When every element serves a purpose, the user experience becomes effortless.
